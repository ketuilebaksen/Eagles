Let me tell you who Chance Campbell is, because a lot of fans do not know much about him.

According to the Eagles' official website, Campbell is a former sixth-round pick of the Tennessee Titans in 2022. He played three college seasons at Maryland and then transferred to Ole Miss for his senior year. He is six foot two and two hundred thirty-two pounds. And according to the Eagles' official website, he spent all of 2025 on the Eagles' practice squad. According to one report, he has played in just five career NFL games and is best known for his work on special teams.

And his journey this season has been a roller coaster. According to the Eagles' official website, he was released during the final roster cuts in August and quickly re-signed to the practice squad. According to one report, during training camp, Vic Fangio told reporters to put Campbell in their projections for the final fifty-three-man roster. And then, according to the Eagles' official website, he returned to the practice squad on November sixteenth. And just over a week later, he has a spot on the fifty-three-man roster.
