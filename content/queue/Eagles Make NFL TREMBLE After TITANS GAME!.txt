To understand how we got here, you have to rewind to the offseason. Safety was the question mark heading into this year. Reed Blankenship, a steady, reliable starter, left in free agency and signed with the Houston Texans on a three-year deal worth about 24.75 million. Then the Eagles traded Sydney Brown to Atlanta. Most people who watched this team closely walked out of the summer thinking safety was going to be okay. Two weeks later, that feeling has changed.

So who are these two guys? They're at completely different points in their careers. Marcus Epps is 30. He came into the league in 2019, landed in Philadelphia that same year, and played here through 2022. He was part of that defense that won the NFC Championship. Then he went to Las Vegas, where his 2024 season ended early. He was with the Patriots in camp in 2025, and then he came back to Philly for a second stint. Last season he mostly played special teams until Mukuba got hurt, and then he stepped in as a starter next to Blankenship. This year he's on a one-year deal worth about 1.55 million. Andrew Mukuba is the other side of the coin. Second-round pick out of Texas. Showed real flashes as a rookie, including interceptions, before an injury knocked him out for a big chunk of the season.
