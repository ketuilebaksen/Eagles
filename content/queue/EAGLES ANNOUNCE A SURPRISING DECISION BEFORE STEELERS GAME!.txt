Let me start with Saquon Barkley, because this is the story everybody is talking about.

According to Athlon Sports, NFL insider Albert Breer of Sports Illustrated said on November thirteenth that he expects the Eagles to look at trading Barkley this coming offseason. Here is exactly what he said. Quote, I do think that there’s going to be an exploration of trading him after this year. End quote.

And he explained why. Quote, Saquon Barkley is going to be going into his tenth season in 2027, and so you’re looking at a guy who’s got a lot of mileage, a lot of injury history. End quote. And according to Heavy, Breer also suggested that Barkley’s timeline might not match up with a roster that is being retooled.

Now, let me be very clear about what this is and what it is not. This is an insider’s expectation about the offseason. It is not a report that a trade is happening. No trade has been made, and no trade can be made until the offseason, because the trade deadline has already passed. So Barkley is going to finish this season in an Eagles uniform.

But you can understand why the conversation is happening. According to Heavy, Barkley has sixty-nine carries for two hundred ninety-six yards and three touchdowns this season, plus seven catches for forty-one yards. And according to the Associated Press, he is averaging forty-two point three rushing yards per game. For a player who ran for more than two thousand yards in the Super Bowl season, those are not the numbers anyone expected.

According to Heavy, he has dealt with stinger, hamstring and back injuries this season. I am not going to speculate about his health beyond that. But it is fair to say this has been a hard year for him physically, and he has kept playing through it.

And according to Athlon Sports, Barkley still has two years remaining on his contract. So if the Eagles ever did decide to move him, it would be a business decision about the future, not a decision forced by his contract running out.

What would a trade even look like? According to Anthony Miller of Heavy, Barkley would likely not bring back a first-round pick, but he could bring back a solid Day 2 pick, which would help the Eagles find his replacement. That is the writer’s opinion, not a report.

And here is the most interesting part. According to Heavy, Barkley’s future could depend a lot on what happens with Nick Sirianni. If Sirianni stays, Barkley could stay part of the plan. If there is a coaching change, the changes could come much faster.

But not everybody agrees that Barkley is on his way out. According to Athlon Sports, writer Michael Kaskey-Blomain said, quote, I would be surprised if Barkley isn’t back in Midnight Green next season. End quote. And he pointed out something important. Right now, the Eagles do not have any other options at running back. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, after the Eagles waived Jaydon Blue this week, Barkley is the only running back on the active fifty-three-man roster.

And let us not forget what Saquon has meant to this franchise. He signed with the Eagles in March of 2024, and in his very first season in Philadelphia, he ran for more than two thousand yards, was named the AP Offensive Player of the Year, and helped bring home the Lombardi Trophy. That is one of the greatest single seasons a running back has ever had. And according to Athlon Sports, Kaskey-Blomain also wrote that Barkley still has gas in the tank when he is healthy, and that the Eagles have simply had bad injury luck this year. That is a fair point. When the quarterback, the right tackle, the center and the top receivers are all missing, it is very hard to judge a running back by his numbers.

And think about who blocks for him now. Lane Johnson is retired. Cam Jurgens is in Baltimore. The line that helped Saquon run for two thousand yards does not exist anymore. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, the Eagles rank thirty-second in yards per game and thirtieth in points per game. A running back does not suddenly forget how to run. Sometimes the holes just are not there.

Here is my take, and this is just my opinion, not a report. I understand the logic behind Breer’s view. Running backs who have a lot of carries on their bodies do not usually get better in their tenth season. And if the Eagles are building toward the future with ten picks in the first two rounds of the next three drafts, a younger running back could make sense. But I would not trade Saquon Barkley just because the calendar says so. This offense has been broken this season for a lot of reasons, and most of them have nothing to do with Barkley. Fix the offensive line, get healthy receivers back, find stability at quarterback, and then see what Saquon can still do. He has earned that.

Now, let me move to the second story, because it is about a player a lot of fans might have overlooked.

According to Ian Rapoport of NFL Media, as reported by Pro Football Talk on November thirteenth, the Eagles are releasing veteran offensive tackle Fred Johnson. That comes right after the Eagles released wide receiver Hollywood Brown, who according to ESPN sources asked for his release.

And Fred Johnson’s story is worth telling. According to the team’s official website, he came into the league as an undrafted free agent out of the University of Florida in 2019. He signed first with the Pittsburgh Steelers, then spent 2019 through 2021 with the Cincinnati Bengals. He played part of 2022 with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers before joining the Eagles’ practice squad.

And in Philadelphia, he found a home. According to the team, he became a reliable swing tackle who could play on both sides. In 2024, he filled in mostly for an injured Jordan Mailata at left tackle. And in 2025, he started the back half of the season in place of Lane Johnson at right tackle. According to Pro Football Focus, as cited by the team, he allowed just two sacks in nearly four hundred pass-blocking snaps last season. That is excellent for a backup tackle.

His path was not easy either. According to the team, he left for the Jacksonville Jaguars in the 2025 offseason, and then the Eagles made a trade to bring him back as their swing tackle before Week 1. And in March of this year, according to the team, he agreed to a one-year deal to stay.

But this season, his role shrank. According to PFT, he played in seven games, with just twenty-four offensive snaps and twenty-two special teams snaps. He has played fifty-eight games for the Eagles with fourteen starts.

And the reason his role shrank has a name. Markel Bell.

According to Dave Zangaro of NBC Sports Philadelphia, Bell is a rookie offensive tackle from Miami. He is six foot nine, he is twenty-one years old, and the Eagles drafted him with the sixty-eighth overall pick in the third round in April. When Lane Johnson suddenly retired in October, Bell took over at right tackle for the rest of the season. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, he made his first NFL start against the Rams on short notice, and he played every single snap.

And the veterans believe in him. According to Bell, Lane Johnson told him, quote, don’t make the game bigger than it is. End quote. Jordan Mailata said, quote, kudos to the rook, man, stepping in and stepping up. End quote. And Howie Roseman said, quote, I think Markel was somebody we were really excited to get here in the third round. End quote.

And according to NBC Sports Philadelphia, this was not a surprise to people inside the building. During training camp, Bell took most of the first-team reps at right tackle while Lane Johnson was away for four days. Johnson reportedly called Bell one of the best rookies he had ever been around. And Mailata said Bell is not afraid of criticism or hard work. But Mailata also gave him some honest advice. He told Bell not to try to fill Lane Johnson’s shoes, because that would be impossible. Just be yourself, and get better every week.

And that is exactly what the final eight games should be about for Bell. He is twenty-one years old, and he is learning on the job against some of the best pass rushers in the world. There are going to be ups and downs. NBC Sports Philadelphia said his first start had some of both. But every snap he takes now is an investment in 2027 and beyond.

So releasing Fred Johnson is not really about Johnson doing anything wrong. This is just my opinion, not a report, but it is about the Eagles committing to Bell and to the next version of their offensive line. And it fits a pattern we have seen all week. Hollywood Brown, Fred Johnson, Jaydon Blue. One-year veterans and backups are being moved out, and young players are getting the snaps.

I want to say thank you to Fred Johnson. He was an undrafted player who bounced around the league and then became a dependable piece of this offensive line when this team needed him. That is the kind of player championship rosters are built with. I wish him the best wherever he goes next.

And quickly on Hollywood Brown, since his release came the same week. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, he signed a one-year, five million dollar deal in March, but he played in only four of nine games and caught five passes for sixty-three yards. According to Yardbarker, the Eagles pointed to the expected returns of DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks, plus the improved play of Darius Cooper and Makai Lemon. So in the span of a few days, the Eagles moved on from a backup running back in Jaydon Blue, a veteran receiver in Brown, and a veteran tackle in Johnson. Three different positions, one clear message. The rest of this season is about finding out who belongs in the future.

Now, the third story. And I warned you, this one is uncomfortable.

According to Ed Kracz of Eagles On SI, in an analysis published on November thirteenth, there is a real question about whether this team can avoid losing every remaining game. The Eagles have lost seven straight games, and they have eight games left. If they lost all of them, that would be fifteen straight losses to end the season.

Now, let me be clear. That is not a prediction. Kracz was examining the chances of avoiding that streak, not saying it will happen. But the numbers are tough. According to Kracz, since their last win on September twentieth at Tennessee, the Eagles have been outscored one hundred seventy-nine to one hundred. That is a point differential of minus seventy-nine during the losing streak.

And here is the frustrating part. According to Kracz, the eight remaining opponents have a combined record of thirty-four wins, thirty-two losses and one tie. That is basically an average schedule. It is not impossible. The Eagles host Pittsburgh on November twenty-second, they go to Dallas on Thanksgiving, they play at Arizona on December sixth, they host Indianapolis on December thirteenth, and they finish with San Francisco on January third and the Giants on January tenth.

And there is a sign of how far this season has fallen. According to Kracz, the Steelers game was originally scheduled for a national four twenty-five broadcast on CBS. It was flexed to one in the afternoon because of how poorly the Eagles have played. That has not happened to this franchise very often in recent years.

But now here is the stat I promised you. The one that might give you some real hope.

According to Kracz, Nick Sirianni is five and zero coming out of bye weeks in his career. In 2021, the Eagles beat Washington twenty-seven to seventeen after the bye. In 2022, they beat Pittsburgh thirty-five to thirteen. In 2023, they beat Kansas City twenty-one to seventeen on Monday Night Football. In 2024, they beat Cleveland twenty to sixteen. And in 2025, they won at Green Bay ten to seven.

Five byes, five wins. And look at who was on that list in 2022. The Pittsburgh Steelers. The same team the Eagles play next.

According to Kracz, Sirianni said, quote, this week gives us a great opportunity to spend extra time, end quote, talking about the bye week. And according to the Associated Press, he said the team is going through its self-scout process. That is the extra time to look at what is going wrong, find the mistakes and try to fix them.

Now, I am not going to pretend that a perfect bye-week record guarantees anything. This team is very different from those teams. Jalen Hurts is out for the season, Lane Johnson has retired, Cam Jurgens and Jordan Davis have been traded, and the offense ranks near the bottom of the league. But if there is one coach who has shown he can use a bye week to get his team ready, it is Sirianni. And if there was ever a time for him to prove it again, it is now.

And remember what Jason Kelce said on 94 WIP this week, according to Heavy. Quote, I think it’s less about wins and losses and more about improvement. End quote. That is the perfect way to look at the Steelers game. Even if the Eagles do not win, fans will be watching to see whether the self-scout actually changed anything. Fewer penalties. Better protection. A cleaner game plan. A team that looks like it knows what it is doing in the second half. If you see that, there is something to build on. If you do not, the questions about the coaching staff will get a lot louder.

And here is the question that is starting to divide Eagles fans. According to the Associated Press, Tankathon projects the Eagles with the number five pick in the 2027 draft right now. And with Jacksonville’s first-round pick also in hand, a top-five pick of their own could help Howie Roseman speed up this rebuild. So some fans are asking, is it worth losing more games for a better pick?

Here is my take, and this is just my opinion, not a report. I will never root for this team to lose. The players do not tank, the coaches do not tank, and nobody in that locker room is trying to lose. Every game matters for the young players trying to earn a future here, for the coaches trying to keep their jobs, and for the culture Jason Kelce talked about this week. If the Eagles win a few more games and fall a few spots in the draft, that is fine with me. Winning builds something too.

Now, let me talk about the Steelers, because this is not an easy game. According to the NFL standings, Pittsburgh is six and two and sitting on top of the AFC North. And according to Kracz, the Steelers are coming off a Sunday night game at Cincinnati. That means the Eagles will have had two weeks to prepare, and the Steelers will be on a normal week. That is exactly the kind of advantage Sirianni has used before.

Here is what I will be watching. First, whether Saquon Barkley gets help in the backfield, with Will Shipley’s first chance to return from injured reserve lining up with this game. Second, whether DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks are back. Third, how Markel Bell handles a strong Steelers pass rush. Fourth, what kind of changes come out of the self-scout. And fifth, whether Sirianni can keep his perfect bye-week record alive.

Alright, Eagles Nation, let me give you both sides.

On one side, the people who think the Eagles should start moving on from veterans like Barkley. They will say Breer is one of the best-connected insiders in the league, and he would not say this without a reason. They will say Barkley will be in his tenth season in 2027, with a lot of mileage and injury history. They will say a Day 2 pick and a younger running back fit a team with ten premium picks over three drafts. They will say releasing Fred Johnson and Hollywood Brown shows the Eagles are already in a full reset. And they will say a perfect record after the bye means nothing for a team that has been outscored by seventy-nine points in seven games.

On the other side, the people who think this is not the time to panic. They will say no trade has been made, and the Eagles still have no other running back on the active roster. They will say Barkley still has two years left on his deal and has been playing through injuries on a broken offense. They will say Markel Bell has earned the right tackle job, so releasing Fred Johnson is about development, not giving up. They will say the remaining schedule is basically average, at thirty-four, thirty-two and one. And they will say Sirianni’s five and zero record after byes, including a win over Pittsburgh, is a real reason to believe the Eagles can end this streak.

And here is where I land. This is just my opinion, not a report. I do not think the Eagles will lose fifteen straight. I think the bye week came at the right time, and I think Sirianni’s history after the bye is real. On Saquon, I understand why an insider expects an exploration of a trade, and I think the Eagles will at least listen to offers this offseason. But I would only trade him if someone blows them away. And on Fred Johnson, I think the move makes sense. Markel Bell is the future at right tackle, and the Eagles need to see as much of him as possible.
