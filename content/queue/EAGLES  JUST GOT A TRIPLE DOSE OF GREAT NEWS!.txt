Let me start with the best news of the week. Jalen Hurts.

According to ESPN’s Tim McManus, Jalen made an appearance at the team’s practice facility on Wednesday. For some players, it was the first time they had seen him since he was placed on injured reserve with blood clots. According to Athlon Sports, Jalen sat in on the team meeting and on more meetings after that. He did not address the team. He did not need to. According to a source cited by McManus, his presence, quote, did provide a morale boost for some. End quote.

Think about that, Eagles Nation. A team that has lost six straight. A locker room that has been hearing about dysfunction and culture and blame for weeks. And then the leader of this team walks into the room. He does not give a speech. He just shows up. That is who Jalen Hurts is. Quiet, steady and present.

And Nick Sirianni made it clear how much Jalen means to him. According to Athlon Sports, Sirianni said he is, quote, in constant communication, end quote, with Jalen, and that he knows Jalen is resting and recovering. And then he said this. Quote, I miss him like crazy. End quote.

That is not a coach talking about a quarterback. That is a man talking about someone he cares about. And it matches what Donovan McNabb said earlier this week, when he told the Eagles Collective podcast that he had talked to Jalen and that, quote, he’s doing well. He’s in good spirits. End quote.

Let me be clear about one thing. Jalen is still out for the season, and nobody has reported anything different. The Eagles and his agent have said his prognosis is very good and that they expect a full recovery. I am not going to speculate about medical details, and I am not going to put a date on anything. But seeing him back in that building, in good spirits, is the kind of news that matters more than any box score. That is great news number one.

Now, great news number two. Saquon Barkley.

On Wednesday, Saquon did not practice. He has been dealing with hamstring and back injuries, and he played just twenty-two snaps against Washington on a pitch count. But on Thursday, according to Eagles Wire, Saquon was upgraded to a limited participant. That is a step in the right direction. It does not tell us how many snaps he will play on Sunday, and I am not going to guess. But after two games of playing banged up, any progress is good to see.

And now the part of the injury report that is not so great. DeVonta Smith missed his second straight practice on Thursday with a hamstring injury. Dontayvion Wicks did not practice with an ankle injury. Jihaad Campbell, Michael Carter the second, Britain Covey and Jonathan Jones also did not practice. Jake Elliott was limited with a right ankle issue. And Tanner McKee was limited on Thursday with a rib injury, after being a full participant on Wednesday with a chest injury.

So yes, there is good news. But there is still a long list of players this team is waiting on.

On the positive side, the offensive line has more bodies. Landon Dickerson, Jordan Mailata and rookie right tackle Markel Bell were all full participants on Thursday, along with Dallas Goedert, Jordan Davis, Moro Ojomo, Zack Baun and Kenny Moore the second.

Now, great news number three, and this is the one that has everyone talking. The Eagles have a new starting quarterback.

On Monday, Nick Sirianni said, quote, Tanner’s our guy. We’re not making any changes there. End quote. And then, on Wednesday, according to ESPN’s Adam Schefter and NFL Network’s Mike Garafolo, the Eagles decided to start Joe Milton the third against the Giants.

Here is why. According to NFL dot com, Tanner McKee started three games after Jalen went down, and the Eagles lost all three. He completed seventy-two of one hundred fourteen passes for six hundred thirty-eight yards, with two touchdowns and two interceptions. Johnny McGonigal of PennLive described McKee as neither terrible nor particularly good. And with a six-game losing streak, the Eagles felt the offense needed a spark.

So who is Joe Milton? He is twenty-six years old. He played his college football at Michigan and Tennessee, and according to the New York Post, he went thirteen and eight as a college starter. The Patriots took him in the sixth round in 2024. In Week 18 of his rookie season, he was not the official starter, but he played most of the game and completed twenty-two of twenty-nine passes for two hundred forty-one yards and a touchdown, and he ran for another score. Then the Patriots traded him to Dallas in 2025, where he backed up Dak Prescott. With the Cowboys last season, he appeared in four games. He did not make the Cowboys’ fifty-three-man roster this summer, and the Eagles signed him off their practice squad on October sixteenth, after Jalen went on injured reserve.

And according to NFL dot com, in those four games with Dallas last season, Milton completed fifteen of twenty-four passes for one hundred eighty-three yards, with a touchdown and two interceptions, and he ran five times for fifty yards. Ten yards a carry. That is a small sample, but it tells you he can move. And there is a little bit of a twist here too. The Eagles found their new starting quarterback on the Dallas Cowboys’ practice squad. If Milton plays well, that is a story Eagles fans will enjoy telling for a long time.

And here is what makes Milton interesting. NFL dot com describes him as a mobile, strong-armed quarterback whose running ability could help an offense that has struggled to block. He is known for a rocket arm. Now, to be fair, he is not known for his accuracy. And he has never made an NFL start. So this is a big gamble. But when you have lost six straight, sometimes a gamble is exactly what you need.

Listen to how Milton talked about the opportunity on the team’s official website. Quote, we all know that you have to be ready to be on the field at all times. So, I’m going to be ready on Sunday. End quote. He said, quote, I’m going to play my game and stay within the offense, and that’s the job. End quote. He said he feels comfortable with the offense. And he said, quote, I’m the oldest of seven kids in my family, so that is something I had to do early in my life. End quote. And then, quote, it’s football, and I’m looking forward to the opportunity to lead this team and play the game I love. End quote.

And I have to give Tanner McKee a lot of credit here. He did not complain. He said, quote, I’m here to help us win, no matter what that means. End quote. He said, quote, I am going to stay ready and learn from the experience and be at my best when my number is called again. End quote. And he said, quote, Joe is doing a great job, and I think he’s going to go out there and play a good game on Sunday. End quote. That is a great teammate, Eagles Nation. That is the kind of player you want in your quarterback room.

Now, here is my take on the quarterback change, and this is just my opinion, not a report. I like it. McKee did his best, and he deserves respect. But the offense needed something different, and Milton gives you something different. A quarterback who can extend plays with his legs and push the ball down the field could take some pressure off this offensive line. Will it work? I do not know. But Sirianni said himself that doing the same thing over and over and expecting a different result is crazy. This time, he backed it up with action.

Now, the opponent. The Giants come to Lincoln Financial Field on Sunday at one in the afternoon Eastern, on FOX. They are four, two and one. According to Sports Illustrated, the Giants are three-and-a-half-point favorites, on the road, in Philadelphia. That tells you everything about where this Eagles team is right now.

But the Giants have their own problems. According to PhillyVoice, their starting quarterback Jaxson Dart is out for the season after a knee injury in Week 2, and J.J. McCarthy has taken over. Pass rusher Brian Burns is also out for the season with a torn ACL. And on Wednesday, running back Cam Skattebo did not practice with a hamstring injury, and Abdul Carter missed practice with an illness. And the Giants are not taking Milton lightly. According to the New York Post, Giants coach John Harbaugh said, quote, we’ll try to anticipate what exactly they’re gonna run with him, but it’s still gonna be their offense. End quote.

Now, let us talk about the offensive line, because this is where the fight is happening this week.

The numbers are ugly. According to Dave Zangaro of NBC Sports Philadelphia, through eight games, the Eagles rank thirtieth in points and thirty-second in total yards. They rank twenty-ninth in pressure rate allowed. And they have allowed a free rusher on eighteen point two percent of pass plays, the most in the league. Against Washington, they allowed ten pressures on twelve blitzes.

And that has put offensive line coach Chris Kuper under the microscope. But Sirianni is standing behind him. According to Zangaro, Sirianni said, quote, Chris is coaching his butt off. End quote. And he said, quote, he’s fighting his butt off, he’s coaching his butt off on a daily basis. End quote. Sirianni pointed out that fans only see Kuper on Sundays, while he sees the work every day. And according to the Inquirer, Jordan Mailata also defended Kuper, saying, in his words, he’s doing everything he can.

And let us be fair to Kuper. This line has been hit hard. Landon Dickerson has played in only three of the eight games, according to NBC Sports Philadelphia. Lane Johnson is no longer in the lineup, and a rookie is starting at right tackle. That is a lot for any coach to deal with.

And a lot of fans noticed another name this week. On Monday, the Saints fired their run game coordinator, T.J. Paganetti. According to EssentiallySports, Paganetti was an assistant offensive line coach for the Eagles during both Super Bowl wins, against the Patriots and against the Chiefs. He coached on the Eagles’ offensive staff from 2017 through 2024, working alongside Jeff Stoutland. And Jason Kelce went on X and said, quote, T.J. is an outstanding coach. He brought a ton of value for us in Philadelphia, and was a big part of our success up front. End quote. And he added, quote, the team that hires him is getting a great one. End quote.

Now, to be clear, nobody has reported that the Eagles are interested in Paganetti. That is fan talk, not a report. But it is easy to see why fans are dreaming about it.

And speaking of Stoutland, there was a rumor this week that got Eagles legends fired up. Earlier this week, Bill Colarulo of 97.5 The Fanatic posted on X that, per his sources, it was not only Nick Sirianni who wanted a change in the run game. He wrote that it was Stoutland’s own players who felt it was time for, quote, a change in scheme and voice. End quote.

Jason Kelce did not like that one bit. According to Crossing Broad, Kelce went on WIP on Thursday and said, quote, I take great offense to somebody alluding to players not fighting really hard for Jeff. End quote. He said the decision to move on from Stoutland, quote, had nothing to do with the players. End quote. He pointed out that Jordan Mailata had dinner with Stoutland after he left, and that when Lane Johnson retired, the only coach he met with was Stoutland. Kelce did say he was not around for Stoutland’s final season, and that the decision was about the organization wanting a different direction. And later on Thursday, Colarulo responded, saying his report was focused mainly on the run game coordinator role, and that the team did not leak it to him.

Here is my take, and this is just my opinion. I do not know who is right, and neither does anyone outside that building. But when Jason Kelce takes great offense to something, I pay attention. And whatever happened with Stoutland, the bigger point is this. The offensive line has not been the same, and fixing it is the most important job in Philadelphia right now.

And all of this is happening with the trade deadline just days away. It is Tuesday, November tenth, at four in the afternoon Eastern, which means the Giants game is the last game before it. ESPN’s Jeremy Fowler has put the odds of Riq Woolen being traded at thirty-five percent and Landon Dickerson at twenty-five percent. The Athletic’s Daniel Popper named Denver as the best trade fit for guard Tyler Steen. And a Boston columnist pitched the Patriots calling about Dallas Goedert. On the other side, Albert Breer reported that the Eagles told teams Moro Ojomo is not available, and Jimmy Kempski wrote that DeVonta Smith isn’t going anywhere. So Sunday is not just about Joe Milton. It is about every veteran on this roster showing Howie Roseman that this group is worth keeping together.
