Let me start with Hurts in his own words.

Back in April 2023, when Hurts signed his contract extension, he spoke about what this city means to him. According to 97.5 The Fanatic, he said, quote, Philadelphia is a very special place and a place I want to be for a very long time. End quote. He talked about wanting, quote, not only to lead this team but to lead this city. End quote. And he said, quote, I love this place, I love the fans, I love the culture that we have here. End quote.

And then he said something that tells you everything about who he is. According to 97.5 The Fanatic, when he talked about the sacrifices of the job, he said, quote, that’s the sacrifice we make because I love this place. End quote. And about how he got here, he said, quote, I couldn’t do it another place and I couldn’t imagine this being another way. End quote. He even explained why Philadelphia is different from anywhere else he has played. Quote, but Philly, it’s a Philly thing. It’s different. End quote.

And then there is the line every Eagles fan remembers from that press conference. According to Sports Illustrated, Hurts said, quote, money is nice, end quote, and, quote, championships are better. End quote.

And he did not just talk about himself. According to Sports Illustrated, Hurts described the Eagles’ building as, quote, full of hungry individuals, end quote, who were willing to do whatever it takes to win. He said his goal has always been, quote, to be the best player that I can be, end quote, and that he loves, quote, the grind, end quote, and, quote, the hustle that it takes. End quote. And on that same day, according to CBS Philadelphia, Jeffrey Lurie called Hurts a culture-setter, and called his extension a special moment for the franchise.

Think about that, Eagles Nation. That is not a player who is looking for the exit. That is a player who told this city he wanted to be here for a very long time, and who said winning championships matters more to him than money. And then he went out and did exactly what he said. According to the Inquirer, in his seven seasons as a starter, the Eagles reached two Super Bowls and won one with Hurts at quarterback. He was the Super Bowl fifty-nine MVP after the 2024 season, when the Eagles beat the Kansas City Chiefs to win the second Super Bowl title in franchise history, a night no Eagles fan will ever forget.

Now, let me tell you about the one contract detail that a lot of people are ignoring.

According to NFL.com, citing Over The Cap, Hurts is in the third season of a five-year, two hundred fifty-five million dollar extension. And according to NFL.com, he has a no-trade clause. That means any trade would need his approval. According to Pro Football Network, citing Spotrac, the deal includes one hundred seventy-nine point three million dollars in total guarantees and a no-trade clause.

So let me be very clear about what that means. Even if somebody in the Eagles’ building wanted to trade Hurts, they could not just do it. He controls where he could go. And when you put that next to what he has said about Philadelphia for years, it tells you he holds the power to stay. And this is just my opinion, not a report, but I believe Jalen Hurts wants to finish what he started here.

And according to 6abc, his contract runs through 2028. His guaranteed money is fifty-one point five million dollars this season, drops to twenty-two million next year and reaches zero in 2028. According to NFL.com, moving on from him would also carry big salary-cap consequences. So keeping Hurts is not just about loyalty. It also makes financial sense.

Now, his health. I want to talk about this the right way.

According to The Athletic, as published by NFL.com, the Eagles and Hurts’s agent, Nicole Lynn, released a joint statement in October after he was diagnosed with blood clots. According to that statement, Lynn said, quote, we expect a full recovery. End quote. And the statement also said, quote, we anticipate that he will have no long-term effects. End quote. According to Eagles Wire, the statement said his prognosis is very good. And according to the statement, he is expected to resume contact football activities once he is off blood thinners.

That is all I am going to say about his health, because that is what the team and his agent have said. He is on injured reserve and is not expected to play again this season. There is no return date, and nobody should be guessing. His recovery comes first. But the message from the people closest to him is clear. They expect him back, and they expect him to be fully healthy.

And the people in the building have made their feelings clear too. According to NFL.com, Nick Sirianni said, quote, I miss him. End quote. And, quote, I just deeply care about him and hope that he continues to improve with what he’s going through. End quote. And owner Jeffrey Lurie said, quote, I love everything about him. End quote.

When the head coach and the owner talk about a player like that, that does not sound like a team getting ready to move on.

And let me be honest about this season too, because I am not going to pretend it was perfect before he got hurt. According to Pro Football Network, in early October, Hurts had seven hundred thirteen passing yards with a fifty-nine point six completion percentage, seven touchdown passes and three interceptions, plus one hundred five rushing yards and a rushing touchdown. Those are not his best numbers. But he was doing it with a new offensive coordinator, in an offense that was already struggling to find its identity. And when he went down, the offense did not get better. It got a lot worse.

Now, let me tell you what is happening at quarterback right now, because it connects to all of this.

According to Pro Football Rumors, citing ESPN’s Adam Schefter, Joe Milton will start against the Steelers on Sunday. It will be his second career start. According to Yahoo Sports, against the Giants, he completed eighteen of thirty-four passes for one hundred sixty-nine yards with no touchdowns and no interceptions, ran nine times for sixty-four yards and scored a two-point conversion. But he also lost a fumble and was sacked seven times in the nineteen to eleven loss.

And here is an interesting detail. According to Jimmy Kempski of PhillyVoice, Tanner McKee was a full participant in practice on Wednesday with his rib injury. So McKee is healthy. But according to Yahoo Sports, Sirianni is not going back to McKee, who went zero and three as the starter. According to ClutchPoints, McKee will be the backup.

Here is my take, and this is just my opinion, not a report. That decision tells you something. The Eagles are not looking for a new long-term starter right now. They are using these final games to evaluate the young players. Milton has a powerful arm, and he deserves a real look. But none of this changes who the franchise quarterback is. Milton and McKee are auditioning to be the backup. Jalen Hurts is still the guy.

And Milton could have a lot more help on Sunday. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, DeVonta Smith returned to practice and is on track to play after missing three games with a hamstring injury, and Dontayvion Wicks is also on track to play after missing three games with an ankle injury. According to Olivia Reiner of the Inquirer, Smith said he feels, quote, one hundred percent, end quote, and, quote, at the end of the day, it’s not over. End quote. According to Inside The Iggles, if Saquon Barkley, Smith and Wicks all play, this could be the Eagles’ healthiest offense since Week 1.

But there is one concern. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, linebacker Zack Baun missed his second straight practice on Thursday with a back injury, and his status for Sunday is in question. If he cannot go, Jihaad Campbell and Jeremiah Trotter Junior would start. According to Kempski, Baun hurt his back against the Giants, and fans should not count on him for Sunday. I am not going to speculate beyond that.

Now, the new addition. According to the team’s official website, the Eagles claimed offensive tackle Ajani Cornelius off waivers from the Dallas Cowboys on Wednesday, one day after Dallas waived him. He went straight onto the active roster. According to the team, he is six foot five and twenty-four years old. He earned first-team All-CAA honors at Rhode Island in 2022 and was a second-team All-American at Oregon. According to Heavy, he started twenty-eight games at right tackle at Oregon and was the two hundred fourth overall pick in the 2025 draft. According to the Cowboys’ official website, Dallas waived him to make room for veteran Nick Leverett after starting guard Tyler Booker hurt his ankle in a forty-six to forty loss to the 49ers.

Now, let me be fair. According to Heavy, Cornelius did not play a single offensive snap in four games with Dallas. So he is a development project, not a starter. But according to Heavy, the Eagles now have Jordan Mailata and rookie Markel Bell as their starting tackles, with Cornelius and John Ojukwu as depth. And according to the Cowboys’ website, Cornelius will likely face his old team in Arlington on Thanksgiving. You know that is going to be fun.

And think about how this connects to Hurts. When Hurts comes back in 2027, the Eagles need an offensive line that can protect him. According to Inside The Iggles, the Eagles have allowed thirty sacks this season, the fourth-most in the league. Building young depth on the offensive line now is exactly what this team needs to do so that Hurts walks into a better situation next year.

Now, the bigger picture. According to Larry Rupp of Eagles On SI, the Eagles have ten picks in the first two rounds of the next three drafts, after the Jordan Davis trade with Jacksonville, the A.J. Brown trade with New England and the Cam Jurgens trade with Baltimore. And according to ESPN’s Adam Schefter, the Eagles brought back TJ Paganetti as a consultant, a coach who, according to NBC Sports Philadelphia, worked on both Super Bowl staffs and was an assistant offensive line coach in 2024, when, according to Athlon Sports, the Eagles set a franchise record with three thousand eight hundred sixty-six rushing yards.

So put it all together. A franchise quarterback with a no-trade clause who has said Philadelphia is where he wants to be for a very long time. A medical statement that expects a full recovery with no long-term effects. Ten premium draft picks to rebuild the roster around him. Young offensive linemen to protect him. And a coaching staff working to fix the running game. This is just my opinion, not a report, but that sounds like the foundation for another championship run, with Jalen Hurts leading it.
