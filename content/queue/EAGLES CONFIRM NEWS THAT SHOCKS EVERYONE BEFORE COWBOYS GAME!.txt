Eagles Nation, it is game day. Tonight, the Philadelphia Eagles host the Dallas Cowboys on Monday Night Football, and in his final press conference before kickoff, Nick Sirianni confirmed something a lot of fans have been waiting to hear. After four straight losses, the Eagles have changed things this week. Not just the game plan. The schedule. The practices. The process. In Sirianni’s own words, everything is on the table. And he also gave updates on DeVonta Smith, Hollywood Brown, Tanner McKee, rookie tight end Eli Stowers and the backup quarterbacks.

This is James with Second Floor, and today we are going through that press conference answer by answer, and then we are going to put it together with the final injury report and everything we know heading into kickoff tonight. Before we start, hit that like button and subscribe to Second Floor, because we are covering this game from the first snap to the final whistle.

Let me start with the biggest answer of the day, because this is the confirmation that matters most.

A reporter asked Sirianni what he could share about the adjustments the team made this week. Not the schemes, but the process. And here is what he said. Quote, your core values remain the same regardless of what is going on around you. End quote. But then he said, quote, everything else, like I’ve said, is on the table. You can adjust schemes, you can adjust schedules, you can adjust practice. End quote. And then, quote, we’ve adjusted some things, because we’re doing everything we can do on our end to be able to go out there and be successful. End quote.

Eagles Nation, think about what that means. Sirianni is a coach who believes deeply in his routines. When a coach like that tells you the schedule changed and practice changed, that is not a small thing. He admitted he was being very general, and he was not going to give away the details. But the message is clear. The Eagles did not just show up this week and hope things would be different. They changed how they work.

And it connects to another answer. A reporter asked about the self-scouting process, which Sirianni loves. He said they self-scout every week, but especially on a longer week, like this one with the Monday night game. He compared it to second half adjustments. Quote, you have to be adjusting throughout the whole game, and you have to be adjusting throughout the whole year. End quote. He said with the extra day, it was a good, productive week to look at some things.

So here is what that tells me. The Eagles took a hard look in the mirror. They looked at what has not been working, especially on offense, and they used the extra day to make changes. Whether those changes show up tonight is the only question that matters.

Now, let us talk about the most important injury question, because Sirianni was asked directly. Are the Eagles considering shutting DeVonta Smith down, since he has not been able to shake the hamstring since the summer?

And Sirianni did not say yes, and he did not say no. He said, quote, I just want him to continue to get better. We’ll see where that goes. End quote. And then he praised Smith in a way that tells you how much respect he has for him. Quote, nobody takes care of their body like DeVonta Smith. He is such a good example to everybody of how to take care of yourself. End quote. He said Smith is the first one in the building, getting treatment and doing his prehab before practice. And then he said something honest. Quote, sometimes the process doesn’t always produce the results you want. End quote.

Eagles Nation, that is a coach who is hurting for his player. Smith came back against Carolina, caught seven passes for seventy-five yards and a touchdown, and then aggravated the hamstring. He has been ruled out tonight, and this will be the third game he has missed this season. Nobody is saying he is being shut down for the year. But nobody is promising a quick return either. I would not be shocked if the Eagles are extra careful with him, especially with the season where it is. Let us just hope he gets fully healthy. Because a healthy DeVonta Smith in December is worth a lot more to this team than a DeVonta Smith playing at seventy percent in October.

Now, the good news. Hollywood Brown.

Sirianni was asked how Brown has handled not getting the playing time he probably expected. Remember, Brown was the only healthy receiver on the inactive list against Carolina. And Sirianni had nothing but praise for him. Quote, I think he is a great pro. Comes to work every day, just working to get better, paying attention in meetings. End quote. And then he told a story. He said Brown made a catch in practice that was one of the best catches he has seen, because of the way he adjusted to the ball. Quote, he’s highly talented, and will bring some great speed to our team. End quote. And he said, quote, I’ve just seen consistency from him of being the same guy every single day regardless of the circumstances. End quote.

Eagles Nation, read between the lines. With DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks both out tonight, Hollywood Brown is going to play. And his head coach just told the world that Brown has stayed professional, kept working and made one of the best catches he has seen in practice. If there was ever a night for Brown to make an impact, it is tonight. That speed could be exactly what this offense has been missing. Defenses have been sitting on short routes against this offense for weeks. One deep ball changes that.

Then a reporter asked about another one of the young receivers, and how the depth chart shakes out with Smith and Wicks out. And Sirianni was fired up. He said this player has been fun to coach, loves football, is really quick and has, quote, phenomenal hands. End quote. And then he said, quote, we want to get him the football. We know he’s one of our playmakers, and he can do it a lot of different ways. End quote. He said this player has a high football IQ, that he is tough, and that, quote, his ceiling is very high because of the talent that he has. End quote. And he finished with this, quote, I’m really excited about him and the opportunities that he’s going to have, this game and leading into the future. End quote.

That tells me the Eagles have a plan to get the ball to their young playmakers tonight. With Makai Lemon, Darius Cooper, Hollywood Brown, Elijah Moore and Britain Covey expected to make up the receiver room, somebody is going to get a real opportunity. Sirianni is basically telling you that the young guys are not just filling in. They are part of the plan.

If you are new to Second Floor, welcome. Drop a comment and tell me, which receiver has the biggest night for the Eagles tonight? Hollywood Brown, Makai Lemon, or somebody else?

Now, the tight ends. Sirianni was asked about rookie Eli Stowers, and how he has looked since returning from injured reserve to practice. He said, quote, he’s had a nice week of practice. End quote. He said Stowers looks fast and athletic, and has done some really nice things. And then he made the room laugh. He said, quote, we’re going to need everybody in the room. End quote. And then he caught himself and said that usually when he says that, he means the team, not the reporters.

Here is why that matters. Stowers is still on injured reserve and was listed as questionable for tonight, along with tight end Grant Calcaterra, guard Landon Dickerson and cornerback Jakorian Bennett. The Eagles can activate any of them until four in the afternoon Eastern on Monday. And according to the Inquirer, if they are not activated this week, they have one more week of practice before the team must either activate them or place them on season-ending injured reserve. So keep an eye on the transactions this afternoon. And with Dallas Goedert available after a full practice on Saturday, the tight end room could be the deepest it has been in weeks.

Now, one of my favorite answers. A reporter asked about the young players who came from winning college programs and are now experiencing the most losing of their lives. How do you deal with the toll that takes?

And Sirianni went back to 2021, his first year in Philadelphia, when the Eagles started two and five. He said he talked to DeVonta Smith, who had barely lost at Alabama, and told him his own college record as a player at Mount Union. Quote, it was sixty-seven and two in five years. End quote. And he told Smith, quote, the NFL is different. There’s more parity in this league than any other league. End quote. He said you can still accomplish all your goals regardless of what happens. And then, quote, now you got to get turned around. Everybody understands that. End quote.

He talked about how the game is different for young receivers in the NFL. Coverage is tighter. You have to win more contested catches. And he said the mental side is a marathon, that young players have to learn not to ride the waves. And then he finished with a line I loved. Quote, more adversity equals stronger growth. End quote. And then he joked that he does not bench press anymore, in case anyone was wondering.

Eagles Nation, remember how that 2021 season ended. The Eagles started two and five, and they made the playoffs. Sirianni is not just telling a story. He is reminding his young players, and all of us, that a bad start does not have to mean a lost season.

Now, Tanner McKee. A reporter pointed out that there were seven weeks between the last time they saw McKee practice in the summer and his first start, and asked how he developed and what made him the number two quarterback. Sirianni said McKee has gotten more and more comfortable in the system. He talked about the developmental periods in practice, where backups get to rep the Eagles’ own plays, not just scout team plays. And then he said, quote, Tanner was the three, but Tanner didn’t budge. He didn’t blink. He continued to stay committed to his process, to our process, and control what he could control. End quote.

That is a big compliment. McKee started this season as the third quarterback. He kept working, moved up the depth chart, and now he is starting on Monday Night Football against the Cowboys. In his first start against Carolina, he went twenty-six of forty-five for two hundred fifteen yards, a touchdown and no interceptions. Now he gets a full week of preparation and a game plan built for him.

And finally, the backup quarterbacks. Sirianni was asked about the backup situation behind McKee. He said they are learning more each day, particularly about Joe Milton and the other young quarterback in the room, and that both are getting scout team reps and developmental reps. He said they bring different skill sets, that both are great athletes, and that both can throw the football and read through progressions. Quote, excited about those guys and excited about what they’ll bring and what they can be. End quote.

Now, nothing in that answer suggests a quarterback change. McKee is the starter tonight. But it is clear the coaches are watching Milton closely.

Now let us put it all together with the final injury report. Ruled out: DeVonta Smith, Dontayvion Wicks, Nolan Smith and Michael Carter the second. Questionable: Saquon Barkley, who was a full participant on Saturday and is expected to play, Jonathan Greenard, who called himself a game-time decision, Jalyx Hunt, and the four players on injured reserve. Dallas Goedert has no designation. And according to Bleeding Green Nation, Drew Kendall is set to return as the starting center, and Landon Dickerson is expected to start at left guard if activated.

On the other side, the Cowboys have ruled out linebacker Dee Winters, returner KaVontae Turpin and tackle Drew Shelton. But they get linebacker DeMarvion Overshown and cornerback Cobie Durant back, according to Yahoo Sports. Dallas is two and four, just like the Eagles, and Dak Prescott has thrown four interceptions in his last two games.

So what does the game plan look like tonight? If I am Sean Mannion, I start with Saquon. Run the ball early, even if the first few carries do not go anywhere, because that is how you open up play action. Then use Goedert as McKee’s safety blanket over the middle. Mix in quick throws to the young receivers so McKee can get the ball out fast against the blitz. And take a couple of shots down the field to Hollywood Brown to keep the Dallas safeties honest. Against Carolina, McKee threw forty-five passes. Tonight, I want that number way lower, and I want the Eagles to win the time of possession battle.

On defense, the edge is the biggest worry. Nolan Smith is out, Greenard may be on a pitch count if he plays, and Jalyx Hunt was limited all week. According to Bleeding Green Nation, Arnold Ebiketie and A.J. Epenesa are the only edge rushers who are sure to play, and the Eagles may elevate Keyshawn James-Newby from the practice squad again. That means Vic Fangio may have to manufacture pressure with blitzes and disguises. And there is a fun chess match here. The Cowboys’ defensive coordinator, Christian Parker, worked under Fangio in 2024 and 2025. The student knows the teacher, and the teacher knows the student.

And remember what is riding on this beyond tonight. According to the Philadelphia Inquirer, the trade deadline is November tenth, and the Eagles play three straight division games before it. Dallas tonight, then Washington, then the Giants. Adam Schefter said on ESPN this week that it is not in Howie Roseman’s DNA to be a seller. But the results of these three games will shape what Roseman does. A win tonight keeps every door open.

And through all of this, Jalen Hurts is still the most important story in this building. He is out for the season with blood clots, and the team says his prognosis is very good and a full recovery is expected. This week, the players union called for a full investigation into his medical care, while the league has backed the Eagles’ handling. Sirianni said he cares deeply about Jalen. Whatever happens tonight, the most important result is Jalen getting fully healthy.

On one side, the skeptics. They will say Sirianni talks about process every single week, and the process has produced four straight losses. They will say everything is on the table sounds great, but fans have not seen anything change on the field. They will say the injury news is still bad, with DeVonta Smith out and no clear timeline, and that a press conference full of praise for backups does not change the fact that the Eagles are starting a backup quarterback with backup receivers in a primetime division game.

On the other side, the believers. They will say this was a coach who admitted things needed to change, and then changed them. Schedules, practice, schemes. They will say the extra day of self-scouting is exactly what this team needed. They will say Sirianni has been here before, starting two and five in 2021 and still making the playoffs. And they will say that with Saquon back, Goedert available and the offensive line getting healthier, the Eagles finally have a few things going their way.

And here is where I land. This is just my opinion, not a report. I liked this press conference. The most important line was not about any one player. It was everything is on the table. That tells me Sirianni is not too proud to change. Now he has to show it. Tonight, I want to see a different offense. More Saquon. More Goedert. A couple of deep shots to Hollywood Brown. Quick throws to the young playmakers. And a defense that makes Dak Prescott pay for his mistakes. If the Eagles do that, I believe they snap this losing streak tonight.
