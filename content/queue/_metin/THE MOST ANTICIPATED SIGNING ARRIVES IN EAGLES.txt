Eagles Nation, there is a new roster move in Philadelphia, and it tells you a lot about where this team is right now. But let me be completely honest with you before we go any further. The Eagles have not made a big signing. There is no star free agent walking through the door, and there is no blockbuster addition. The real news actually goes the other way. According to Jordan Schultz of the Schultz Report, as reported by Reuben Frank of NBC Sports Philadelphia, the Eagles are releasing veteran wide receiver Hollywood Brown. That is a player leaving, not a player arriving. And while we are setting the record straight, you may have seen videos claiming that ESPN has confirmed Nick Sirianni is about to be fired. That is not true. There has been no official decision and no report from ESPN that Sirianni is being fired. What we do have is a team at two and seven, a lot of honest conversation about the head coach’s future, and some very interesting comments from Eagles legend Jason Kelce.

Let me start with Hollywood Brown.

According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, Jordan Schultz posted the news on Thursday night that the Eagles are releasing Brown, and that the decision was mutual. According to Athlon Sports, Schultz said, quote, both sides agreed it would be beneficial for all involved. End quote. And according to Athlon, Schultz also reported that Brown is expected to land with a playoff contender.

Now, there is a small detail here that matters. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, because this is happening after the trade deadline, Brown is subject to the waiver claim process. And since he has seven years of pension credit, the process works a little differently for him than it would for a younger player. But the bottom line is simple. His time in Philadelphia is over.

So how did we get here? According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, the Eagles signed Brown to a one-year, five million dollar deal in March. According to Athlon Sports, the deal was worth up to six and a half million dollars. Either way, the plan was clear. After the Eagles traded A.J. Brown to the New England Patriots, they expected Hollywood Brown to be a significant part of the passing game next to DeVonta Smith.

It never worked out. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, Brown played in just four of the Eagles’ nine games. He was targeted nine times, he caught five passes for sixty-three yards, and he ran the ball twice for twelve yards. His longest catch was a nineteen-yarder from Tanner McKee in the loss to Dallas, the game where he had his season-high of thirty receiving yards. According to Athlon Sports, he also missed time with an ankle injury, and he was a healthy scratch for the Week 6 loss to the Panthers.

And that is surprising when you look at his career. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, Brown was a first-round pick in 2019. He had a thousand-yard season with Baltimore in 2021, and more than seven hundred yards in both 2020 and 2022. Just last season with Kansas City, he had forty-nine catches for five hundred eighty-seven yards and five touchdowns. For his career, he has three hundred seventy-eight catches for four thousand three hundred eighty-five yards and thirty-three touchdowns.

And Brown was honest about how hard this season has been. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, he expressed frustration with his limited role. He said he expected a bigger role, but he understood that things happen. When he was asked how he was handling it, he said, quote, some days better than others. End quote. He also said he did not want to add stress to the team, and that he tries to stay positive for his family.

Eagles Nation, I respect that. A lot of veterans in that situation would have made noise. Brown did not. He kept quiet, he did his job when he was asked to, and now both sides get a fresh start.

Now, what does this mean for the receiver room?

According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, the healthy wide receivers on the active roster right now are rookie first-round pick Makai Lemon, second-year undrafted free agent Darius Cooper, and Elijah Moore. And the practice squad receivers are Erik Ezukanma and Xavier Gipson. That is a thin group.

But here is the good news. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks could both return after the bye week. And look at the receiving leaders this season, according to NBC Sports Philadelphia. Lemon has thirty-seven catches for three hundred twelve yards and one touchdown. Smith has twenty-six catches for three hundred ten yards and two touchdowns. Wicks has nineteen catches for two hundred eighty-four yards and one touchdown. And Darius Cooper has thirteen catches for two hundred yards and four touchdowns.

Think about that last number. Darius Cooper, a second-year undrafted free agent, leads the Eagles’ receivers in touchdown catches with four. That is exactly why moving on from Brown makes sense. The Eagles need to find out who can help them next season. Lemon is a rookie. Cooper is a young undrafted player who keeps making plays. Those are the guys who need the snaps, not a veteran on a one-year deal who was not part of the plan.

Here is my take, and this is just my opinion, not a report. This move is not about Hollywood Brown being a bad player. It is about the Eagles finally admitting this season is about evaluation. If you are two and seven, every snap should go to a player who might be part of the future. And with Smith and Wicks possibly coming back for the Steelers game, there was just no room left for Brown.

Now, let us talk about that open roster spot, because there is another position that badly needs help.

According to the team, the Eagles waived running back Jaydon Blue on Tuesday. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, that left Saquon Barkley as the only running back on the active fifty-three-man roster. And now, with Brown gone, the Eagles have even more flexibility to add someone.

So who could it be? Let me tell you what we know, without guessing. According to Fantasy Footballers, Will Shipley was placed on injured reserve in October with a partial tear of the plantar fascia in his foot, and his first chance to come back is the Steelers game on November twenty-second. Tank Bigsby, according to Fantasy Footballers, had core muscle surgery and has already been eligible to return since the Giants game. Neither one has been activated yet, and eligible does not mean ready. That is a decision for the team and the doctors.

There are also two running backs on the practice squad. According to the team, Elijah Mitchell was elevated from the practice squad for the Giants game. And then there is a name a lot of fans do not know yet. According to the team’s official website, the Eagles signed running back Ian Wheeler to the practice squad on October fifteenth.

And Wheeler’s story is incredible. According to the team, he played four seasons at Howard, rushing for one thousand one hundred forty-three yards and fourteen touchdowns, at six and a half yards per carry. He went undrafted to the Chicago Bears in 2024, and he was featured on Hard Knocks. And here is the part I love. According to the team, he was accepted into Howard’s College of Medicine, but he deferred it to chase his NFL dream. He hurt his knee in the Bears’ final preseason game of 2024, he was waived in 2025, and he spent a month on the Saints’ practice squad. Then he went to the UFL, joined the Louisville Kings, won the 2026 UFL title, and was named United Bowl MVP, with eighty-one yards and a touchdown at eight point one yards per carry. He spent this year’s training camp and preseason with the Buffalo Bills before landing in Philadelphia.

So if you want to call anything the most anticipated arrival right now, this is just my opinion, not a report, but it is the return of a healthy running back to help Saquon. Whether that is Shipley, Bigsby, Mitchell or even Wheeler, the Eagles cannot keep asking Barkley to carry the entire rushing attack by himself.

Now, let me get to the biggest topic of the week, and that is Nick Sirianni.

Again, there has been no decision. Sirianni has not been fired, and the Eagles have not announced any coaching change. But it is fair to say the pressure is real. According to ESPN’s Tim McManus, in an article published on November seventh, the debate over whether Sirianni will be swept up in an offseason change, or whether he will oversee it, is, quote, well underway. End quote. McManus compared this season to the final years of Andy Reid, Chip Kelly and Doug Pederson, and wrote, quote, Lurie will be watching. End quote. That is analysis, not a report of a decision.

And McManus also gave the other side. According to his reporting, Jeffrey Lurie signed Sirianni to a multi-year extension in May 2025, believed to be worth around fifteen million dollars per year, which gives the owner a financial reason to keep him. And Sirianni said, quote, I’ve always felt nothing but complete support from Mr. Lurie in everything. End quote.

Now look at what Sirianni has accomplished. According to ESPN, he made the playoffs in each of his first five seasons, reached two Super Bowls, and won the title after the 2024 season. Entering 2026, his career winning percentage of point six nine four was the third-highest in the Super Bowl era for coaches with at least fifty games, behind only John Madden and Vince Lombardi. According to Heavy, his overall record now stands at sixty-one wins and thirty-three losses.

But this season has been brutal. According to the Associated Press, the seven-game losing streak is the Eagles’ longest since an eight-game skid in 2012. Their playoff odds have fallen to zero point two percent. And Tankathon projects them with the number five pick in the draft.

And the offense is the main problem. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, the Eagles rank thirty-second in yards per game, thirtieth in points per game and twenty-ninth in passing yards per game. According to the Associated Press, they have been outscored one hundred twenty-six to forty in the second half of games this season, and Saquon Barkley is averaging forty-two point three rushing yards per game.

And the running game has been a big part of it. According to Yahoo Sports, the Eagles are averaging just three point seven rushing yards per play on two hundred thirteen attempts. For a team built around Saquon Barkley and a physical offensive line, that number tells you how much has changed this season. And according to the Associated Press, Sean Mannion is in his first season as offensive coordinator, and the offense he has overseen has been one of the worst in the league. The AP also noted that rookie quarterback Cole Payton, a 2026 fifth-round pick, could get a look at some point this season.

This is not the first time Sirianni has faced a collapse, either. According to ESPN, the 2023 team started ten and one, then lost six of its last seven games and lost thirty-two to nine to Tampa Bay in the wild-card round. A lot of people wanted him gone then. Lurie kept him, and two seasons later, the Eagles won the Super Bowl. That history is part of why this decision will not be made quickly. And the players still believe in him. According to ESPN, Saquon Barkley, talking about his head coach, said, quote, Nick is that to a T. End quote.

According to the Associated Press, Sirianni said during the bye week, quote, we’re going through our self-scout process. End quote. And about his job, he said, quote, my job is to do everything I can do to turn this around and fix this. End quote.

Now, here is where Jason Kelce comes in.

According to Heavy and Yahoo Sports, Kelce went on the 94 WIP Morning Show, and his comments were posted on November twelfth. And what he said was not what a lot of people expected. He did not say fire Sirianni, and he did not say keep him no matter what. He said, quote, I think it’s less about wins and losses and more about improvement. End quote.

Then he laid out the real question. Quote, do we want to have Nick Sirianni and trust him to right this ship the following year? End quote.

And he gave Sirianni his credit. Quote, I think, obviously, Nick’s track record is incredible. End quote. And, quote, he’s the most winning coach in the history of the Philadelphia Eagles in this timeframe. End quote.

But Kelce did not hide from the problems either. Quote, this season, though, is not going well. End quote. And, quote, they haven’t made these corrections. End quote.

And according to Heavy, he also talked about what the job really is. He said, quote, it’s the main, main job of the head coach, end quote, to, quote, sustain hope and to build a culture. End quote.

And on his New Heights podcast with his brother Travis, according to the Inquirer, Jason described the trade deadline this way. Quote, we’re in Howie season mode. Unfortunately, it’s not the Howie season we’re used to seeing. End quote. And he explained the new direction, quote, we are now trying to build assets and prep for the future as well as we can, as well as keeping really good players home. End quote.

Eagles Nation, I think Kelce nailed it. This is just my opinion, not a report. The final eight games are not about the record anymore. The playoffs are basically gone. These games are about one question. Is this team getting better? Are the mistakes getting fixed? Are the young players improving? Is the offense finding answers? If the answer is yes, Sirianni has a real case. If the answer is no, Lurie and Roseman will have a very hard decision to make in January.

Now, let me go back to what Kelce said about keeping really good players home, because that connects directly to the trade deadline.

According to the Inquirer, the Eagles made only one trade before the deadline, sending Jordan Davis to Jacksonville for a 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick. But three important players with expiring contracts stayed.

The first is Dallas Goedert. According to the Inquirer, trading him before the deadline would have roughly doubled his dead cap hit to eighteen point two million dollars. He took a pay cut to stay in 2025, and he came back on a one-year deal this year. He is a veteran voice in a young locker room, and rookie tight end Eli Stowers still needs time to develop. Keeping Goedert gives the Eagles options.

The second is Moro Ojomo. According to NFL insider Jordan Schultz, as reported by Heavy, the Jaguars originally asked about Ojomo before turning to Davis, and the Eagles said no. According to Eagles On SI, since the start of 2025, Ojomo has sixty-two tackles, six sacks and ten tackles for loss in twenty-six games. And according to PhillyVoice, he is about to get paid. Keeping Ojomo instead of Davis was a clear vote of confidence.

And the third is Riq Woolen. According to the Inquirer, because Woolen is on a one-year deal, his eight point six million dollar dead cap hit in 2027 would not have changed with a trade. He has not been the shutdown corner fans hoped for, and the penalties have been frustrating. But the Eagles still need a long-term answer at cornerback opposite Quinyon Mitchell. If Woolen plays well over the final eight games, he could make his case to stay.

None of those new contracts are guaranteed. But keeping those three players gives Howie Roseman options for 2027. And it fits exactly what Kelce said. Build assets, prep for the future, and keep really good players home.

And that brings us to the Steelers.

According to the Inquirer, the Eagles host Pittsburgh on Sunday, November twenty-second, at one in the afternoon Eastern on CBS. Then they travel to Dallas for Thanksgiving on November twenty-sixth.
