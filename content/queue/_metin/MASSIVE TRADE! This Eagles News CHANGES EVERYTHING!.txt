Let me start with the trade the Eagles already made, because it changed this season.

On October seventh, one day after Lane Johnson announced his surprise retirement, the Eagles traded center Cam Jurgens to the Baltimore Ravens. According to NFL dot com, the Eagles sent Jurgens and a 2027 seventh-round pick to Baltimore for a 2027 fifth-round pick and a 2028 second-round pick. At the time, according to Newsweek, Adam Schefter said it was not a white flag in Philadelphia. He said the Eagles had moved Drew Kendall to center and that they, quote, like him an awful lot. End quote. But a month later, with this offensive line struggling, that trade is still being talked about all over Philadelphia.

And look at what has happened around the league since then. According to ESPN’s trade tracker, the Cardinals sent Marvin Harrison Junior to the Texans on October eleventh. On October twenty-seventh, the Colts traded DeForest Buckner to the 49ers and Quenton Nelson to the Seahawks. On October twenty-ninth, the Titans sent Jeffery Simmons to the Bears for two first-round picks and a fourth-round pick. And on November fourth, the Ravens added quarterback Jacoby Brissett from the Cardinals. And remember, the Giants’ own quarterback, J.J. McCarthy, came over in a trade with the Vikings back in September.

That tells you something. This is a deadline where teams are willing to make big moves. Think about the Simmons deal alone. Two first-round picks for a defensive tackle. If contenders are paying that kind of price for the interior of the defensive line, you can understand why teams keep calling about Moro Ojomo, and why the Eagles are telling them he is not available. Contenders are paying real prices for players who can help them now. And that is exactly why the Eagles’ phones are ringing.

And the Eagles have already been reshaping the bottom of their roster this week. On Tuesday, they waived cornerback Jakorian Bennett from injured reserve, the player they acquired from the Raiders before last season, in a trade that sent defensive tackle Thomas Booker to Las Vegas. And according to Inside The Iggles, citing Dave Zangaro of NBC Sports Philadelphia, the Eagles reached an injury settlement with running back Dameon Pierce, making him a free agent. Those are not blockbusters. But they show you that Howie Roseman is already cleaning house where he can.

Now, let us talk about the Eagles who could be on the move.

According to Larry Rupp of Eagles On SI, there are three potential trade candidates who must be showcased against the Giants today.

The first is Moro Ojomo. He is in the final year of his rookie contract, and the Eagles have already extended two other defensive tackles in Jordan Davis and Jalen Carter. According to Rupp, Ojomo has played under sixty percent of the defensive snaps in three of the last four games, and he does not have a sack yet this season. Rupp wants to see his snaps closer to seventy or eighty percent. But here is the important part. According to Albert Breer of Sports Illustrated, the Eagles have told teams Ojomo is not available, and Howie Roseman has signaled a plan to extend him rather than trade him. And according to Pro Football Rumors, citing ESPN’s Jeremy Fowler, the Jacksonville Jaguars are among the teams that have Ojomo on their radar. So the interest is real. The question is whether the Eagles mean it when they say he is staying.

And it makes sense that teams want him. Defensive tackles who can rush the passer and stop the run are some of the hardest players to find in this league, and Ojomo is only a former seventh-round pick on a rookie contract. That is exactly the kind of value contenders love at the deadline.

And Ojomo made it clear how he feels. According to Eagles On SI, he said, quote, Howie and Lurie mean so much to me for giving me this opportunity to play for this team. End quote.

The second name is Hollywood Brown. He is twenty-nine years old, and according to Rupp, an ankle injury and the depth at receiver kept him out of most of the first six games. But with DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks hurt, Brown has played more than half of the offensive snaps over the last two weeks. He has four catches for forty-seven yards recently. Over his career, he has three hundred seventy-five catches for four thousand three hundred sixty-nine yards and thirty-three touchdowns in ninety-three games. And Rupp makes a great point. Joe Milton’s big arm and mobility are a perfect fit for Brown’s deep speed. A big day today could turn into a late-round pick from a contender.

The third name is defensive tackle Byron Young. He is twenty-five, on an expiring deal, and according to Rupp, he played only fifteen defensive snaps in Week 8. Rupp argues that giving him a bigger role would let other teams evaluate him, and that trading him for a late-round pick should be a priority. It would also open up snaps for second-year defensive tackle Ty Robinson.

Now, the name that has everyone talking. Landon Dickerson.

According to Heavy, Ryan Fowler of NBC Sports Philadelphia wrote on Wednesday, quote, Landon Dickerson continues to draw interest from teams looking for help up front, per source. End quote. And, quote, conversations over the last few days suggest multiple teams have expressed interest in acquiring the veteran guard. End quote. Adam Schefter said on ESPN’s The Aftermath, according to ClutchPoints, quote, Landon Dickerson, I know some teams have been interested in. End quote. And ESPN’s Jeremy Fowler has put Dickerson’s odds of being traded at twenty-five percent.

But a Dickerson trade would be complicated. According to ClutchPoints, he is on a long-term deal that runs through 2028, with void years through 2032. And according to the Inquirer, the Eagles could already lead the NFL in dead cap money in 2027, and a fire sale could make that even worse. So if Dickerson goes, it would have to be for a very good price.

And Dickerson’s answer when reporters asked him about the trade rumors? According to Eagles On SI, he said, quote, it’s really not up to me. End quote. And on 94 WIP, according to Heavy, he said, quote, I love this city, it’s my home. End quote. And, quote, I really don’t want to play for another team. End quote. And when reporters pushed him again before the game, he said his focus was on the Giants.

And there are other names too. Fowler has put the odds of Riq Woolen being traded at thirty-five percent. The Athletic’s Daniel Popper named Denver as the best trade fit for guard Tyler Steen. And a Boston columnist pitched the Patriots calling about Dallas Goedert, though the Inquirer says Goedert’s situation is less likely to change because of the salary cap. But here is the big one. According to PhillyVoice, multiple reports from the ESPN and NFL Network media family said the Eagles will not trade Saquon Barkley. And ESPN’s Jeremy Fowler said his sense is that the Eagles, quote, could be trading some players but likely won’t engage in a rebuild. End quote.

Let me pause here. Comment number one if you would trade Hollywood Brown and Byron Young but keep Ojomo and Dickerson. Comment number two if you would keep everybody.

Now, let us talk about today’s game, because it could decide a lot.

Joe Milton the third makes the first start of his NFL career today. Three weeks ago, he was on the Dallas Cowboys’ practice squad. And listen to how he talked this week. He said he is willing to do whatever the team needs, whether that is running, throwing or handing the ball off. When he was asked how he found out he was starting, he said he just came in, worked and approached the days like he always does, and the coaches told him he was going to start. And then he said this. Quote, just be where my feet is. End quote. He said he loves seeing people around him smile, and that his job is to know his assignment, execute whatever play call comes through his helmet, and make a play.

And Nick Sirianni explained on Friday why he kept the change quiet after saying Tanner’s our guy on Monday. According to Inside The Iggles, citing Zach Berman of The Athletic, Sirianni said, quote, every second that goes by in a situation like that, that you can hold onto information as long as you possibly can. End quote. And he said, quote, my job is to win football games. End quote. Some fans accept that. Others think it was handled poorly. Either way, today is the day we find out if the move was worth it.

I love that energy, Eagles Nation. This team needs a spark, and it sounds like Milton is ready to bring one.

And his teammates are excited too. According to the Inquirer, Dallas Goedert said Milton, quote, throws it hard, end quote, but it is a, quote, really catchable ball. End quote. And Goedert said, quote, if he gets pressure, he can roll out and keep the play alive. End quote. That matters, because according to NBC Sports Philadelphia, the Eagles have allowed a free rusher on eighteen point two percent of pass plays this season, the most in the league.

But Milton will be shorthanded. According to NBC10 Philadelphia, the Eagles ruled out DeVonta Smith, Dontayvion Wicks, Jihaad Campbell, Britain Covey, Michael Carter the second and Jonathan Jones. Tanner McKee is questionable with a rib injury. So this is a big opportunity for rookie Makai Lemon, who has led the Eagles in catches and receiving yards in each of the last two games, and for rookie tight end Eli Stowers, who caught three passes for fifty-three yards against Washington.

Now, the Giants’ side. According to the Giants’ official website, running back Cam Skattebo is doubtful with a hamstring injury, and receiver Charlie Jones is questionable with a hand injury. Malik Nabers has a knee injury, but he practiced fully on Friday and has no game status, so he is expected to play. Pass rusher Abdul Carter also practiced fully after missing Wednesday with an illness. And remember, Jaxson Dart and Brian Burns are out for the season, so J.J. McCarthy is their quarterback.

And here is how much respect the Giants are getting. According to the Inquirer, the Eagles are home underdogs for the first time since 2009. The Giants are favored by three. And every Inquirer writer picked the Giants to win.

And here is a number that tells you how much this season has gone wrong. According to the Inquirer, the Eagles’ point differential through eight games is minus sixty-five or worse, something this franchise has not seen since 1998. Only the winless Chargers are worse. And the Inquirer ran a Save or Trade poll with its readers, and fans voted to trade nearly every player they were asked about. Only three were saved. That is how frustrated this city is right now.

But there was also a reason to smile this week. According to ESPN’s Tim McManus, Jalen Hurts visited the team facility on Wednesday and sat in on meetings, and his presence, quote, did provide a morale boost for some. End quote. Sirianni said, quote, I miss him like crazy. End quote. Jalen is out for the season, and the team expects a full recovery. But if this team needed a reminder of what it is playing for, Jalen walking into that building was it.

Now, the Saquon debate. Saquon is expected to play today. According to NBC10, he missed Wednesday’s practice, then practiced on a limited basis on Thursday and Friday with his hamstring and back injuries. And according to Inside The Iggles, he is expected to be on a snap count and split carries with Elijah Mitchell.

And this is where fans are split. Some say you have to play him, because he is the heart of this offense and the run game is the best way to help a first-time starter. Others say the season is slipping away, so why risk a hamstring and back injury getting worse? I am not going to speculate about medical details, and I am not going to blame anyone in that building. The team has cleared him to play. But this is my opinion, not a report. With the bye week coming right after this game, the Eagles should be very careful with how many snaps they give him. The goal is a healthy Saquon for the rest of this season and for 2027.

Here is what I will be watching today. First, the run game. If Saquon and Mitchell can get going, Milton will have a much easier day. Second, the pre-snap penalties. The Eagles cannot afford another day like Washington, when a false start and an illegal formation wiped out points. Third, the deep shots. Milton has one of the biggest arms in the league, and I want to see Sean Mannion let him use it, maybe even to Hollywood Brown. And fourth, Ojomo and Byron Young on defense. If they get more snaps, that could tell you a lot about what the Eagles are thinking before Tuesday.

Alright, Eagles Nation, let me give you both sides.

On one side, the people who say sell. They will say the Eagles are two and six, home underdogs for the first time since 2009, and already rebuilding the offensive line after the Jurgens trade. They will say Hollywood Brown and Byron Young are on expiring deals and do not fit the future, so get what you can for them. They will say contenders are paying real prices this year, like the Bears did for Jeffery Simmons, and that the Eagles should take advantage. And they will say Ojomo might leave in free agency anyway.

On the other side, the people who say hold. They will say Fowler is right, and the Eagles are not going to rebuild. They will say Ojomo is young and improving, and that Howie Roseman has already signaled he wants to extend him. They will say trading Dickerson would mean a big dead cap hit and leave the line in pieces. They will say Saquon is not going anywhere. And they will say that if Joe Milton gives this team a spark today, the Eagles could go into the bye with momentum and a reason to keep this group together.

And here is where I land. This is just my opinion, not a report. I would listen on Hollywood Brown and Byron Young, because they are not part of the long-term plan, and getting a pick for them would be smart business. But I keep Ojomo and I keep Dickerson. They are core pieces, and trading them would make 2027 harder, not easier. And I keep Saquon. As for today, I want to see Joe Milton play free. Let him run. Let him throw it deep. And let this team play like it has nothing to lose, because honestly, it doesn’t.
