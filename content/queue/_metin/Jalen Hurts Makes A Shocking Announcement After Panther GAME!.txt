On Monday, October nineteenth, the Eagles and Nicole Lynn released a joint statement. In plain English, a clot in a deep vein of his leg, and clots that traveled into both lungs.

It said his treatment should only involve blood thinners. And it said, quote, Jalen’s prognosis is very good, and we expect a full recovery. End quote. The team also said it anticipates no long-term effects, that he will not be allowed to take part in any contact activities, including football, while he is on blood thinners, and that he will remain active around the team and keep conditioning.

The statement itself never used the word season. But Ian Rapoport and Adam Schefter both reported that Hurts is expected to be out for the rest of the year, and Rapoport reported that the treatment will take at least three months.
