On Thursday, the Eagles announced that Jalen Hurts will miss Sunday’s game against the Panthers because of a superficial blood clot in his calf. And I want to walk you through exactly what the team said, because I do not want any speculation on this channel when it comes to a player’s health.

According to the team’s statement and reporting from NFL.com and the Philadelphia Inquirer, here is the timeline. Hurts got cleated in the calf during the Week 4 loss to the Rams on October fourth. On Thursday, October eighth, he went to the training room after practice asking for treatment on that calf. He practiced fully that Friday. Then on Saturday, after the team arrived in London, an ultrasound found a small superficial blood clot. The medical staff concluded he could safely play, and he did, without limitation, against Jacksonville. Doctors recommended a follow-up ultrasound within seventy-two hours of the game and the flight home. He got treatment on Monday and Tuesday, he practiced fully on Wednesday, and then on Wednesday night, the follow-up ultrasound showed movement in that small clot. That is why the team decided to hold him out.

The team said he will sit out as they determine the appropriate course of treatment, and that they are in ongoing contact with the league office. And they finished with this line, quote, His health and safety are our highest priority. End quote.
