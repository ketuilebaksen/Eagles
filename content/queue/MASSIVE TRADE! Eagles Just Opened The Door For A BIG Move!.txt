Let me introduce you to him, because his story is a wild one. Xavier Legette was the 32nd overall pick in the 2024 draft. The very last pick of the first round. Out of the University of South Carolina. And when he got to Carolina, he came with a heavy label. He was supposed to be the go-to target for Bryce Young, the young quarterback the Panthers were building around.

Why the hype? Because physically, this guy is rare. He's about 6 foot 1, around 220 pounds, built more like a running back than a receiver. And at the combine, he ran the 40-yard dash in 4.39 seconds. Four point three nine at 220 pounds. That's the kind of number that makes scouts drop their stopwatches.

And how he got to the first round is just as fascinating as what happened after. Legette spent five years at South Carolina. For the first four, he was basically a role player. In 41 games, he had just 42 catches, 423 yards and five touchdowns. That's it. Then in his final season, 2023, everything exploded. 71 catches, 1,255 yards, seven touchdowns, including a 217-yard game, and a spot on the All-SEC second team. One giant season, and suddenly he was a first-round talent.

The Panthers believed so much that they traded up with the Buffalo Bills to grab him at 32. He was the seventh receiver taken in that draft, and he signed a fully guaranteed four-year deal worth about 12.35 million dollars. Think about the size of that bet. Carolina didn't just pick Legette. Carolina went and got him.

But the NFL didn't follow the script. As a rookie in 2024, Legette played 16 games, started 13, and finished with 49 catches, 497 yards and four touchdowns. Modest numbers for a first-rounder. In 2025, it got worse. 35 catches, 363 yards and three touchdowns in 15 games. And this season? Just two catches. They went for 64 yards, including a 55-yarder against Atlanta. So the big-play ability is still in there. But two catches is two catches.

Why the drop? It has a first and last name. Actually, two of them. The Panthers drafted Tetairoa McMillan in the first round in 2025, and he became their number one receiver.
