Eagles Nation, you may have seen headlines this weekend saying the Eagles made a massive trade and are signing a dream head coach to replace Nick Sirianni. Let me stop you right there, because that is not true. The Eagles have not made a trade, and they cannot make one until the offseason, because the trade deadline has already passed. And the Eagles have not hired a new head coach. Nick Sirianni is still the head coach of the Philadelphia Eagles, and he will be on the sideline against the Pittsburgh Steelers. So what is actually going on? A popular Philadelphia sports radio host named the coach he would want if the Eagles ever made a change, and it is a young offensive mind with deep roots right here in Bucks County. On top of that, a new analysis named three college quarterbacks the Eagles should already be watching for the 2027 draft. And there is real news on two of the Eagles’ best receivers. So let me give you the real story.

Let me start with where this all came from.

According to EssentiallySports, as published on Yahoo Sports on November fourteenth, 94 WIP host Jack Fritz was asked who he would want as the Eagles’ next head coach if they made a change. And his answer was Grant Udinski, the offensive coordinator of the Jacksonville Jaguars.

Here is exactly what Fritz said. Quote, I want someone who has a chance to really be that guy. A guy that can be a play caller. End quote. And about Udinski, he said, quote, I think he has a chance to be the next Ben Johnson, the next Kyle Shanahan, the next Sean McVay. End quote. And he finished with, quote, I want a real head coach. I want a real offense. End quote.

Now, let me be very clear. That is a radio host’s opinion. It is not a report. Nobody has reported that the Eagles have contacted Udinski, interviewed him or have any plans to hire him. Sirianni has not been fired. This is a fan conversation, the kind of conversation that happens in every city when a team is two and seven.

But I get why the name is interesting, so let me tell you who Grant Udinski is.

According to the Inquirer, Udinski grew up in Doylestown, in Bucks County, and he went to Central Bucks West, the same high school his father and mother attended. So he is a local kid. According to the Inquirer, he played college football at Davidson as a tight end and at Towson as a defensive lineman. And he is only thirty years old.

His path to the NFL is one of the most unusual you will ever hear. According to the Inquirer, he got into coaching by networking and visiting Baylor in 2019, where he was later hired as a graduate assistant. Then he followed Matt Rhule to the Carolina Panthers as a coaching assistant for two seasons. In 2022, he joined the Minnesota Vikings as an assistant to head coach Kevin O’Connell. In 2023, he became assistant quarterbacks coach, and in 2024, he became assistant offensive coordinator. And in 2025, he became the offensive coordinator of the Jacksonville Jaguars under Liam Coen. He told the Inquirer, quote, I don’t know that I ever thought I was going to coach in the NFL. End quote.

And other teams have noticed him. According to the Inquirer, Udinski interviewed for the head coaching jobs in Cleveland and Buffalo. According to EssentiallySports, he withdrew from the Browns’ search to stay in Jacksonville, and the Browns hired Todd Monken. The Bills interviewed him too, but promoted Joe Brady.

And look at what the Jaguars are doing this season. According to the NFL standings, Jacksonville is six and two. Fritz suggested Udinski could be one of the minds behind that record, without taking credit away from Coen. He also talked about how Trevor Lawrence has developed under Coen, calling him, quote, the guy that I think we hoped when he was drafted number one overall. End quote. And according to EssentiallySports, Fritz pointed to the praise from Kevin O’Connell, his old boss in Minnesota, and to Jaguars star Travis Hunter, who said, quote, this guy commands a room. End quote.

And according to EssentiallySports, at a press conference in May, Udinski acknowledged he still had a lot of things to improve, and said his focus was on running the ball better and helping his team win games. I like that. A young coach who knows he is not a finished product.

Now, here is the part a lot of those headlines leave out. There is one big question about Udinski, and it is about play-calling.

According to the Inquirer, Udinski works closely with Liam Coen, but Coen is the offensive play-caller in Jacksonville. And according to Jimmy Kempski of PhillyVoice, that is the main concern with Udinski as a head coach candidate. He is not the primary play-caller. So when Fritz says he wants a play-caller, the honest answer is that we have not really seen Udinski call an NFL offense on his own yet.

And Udinski is not the only name out there. According to PhillyVoice, Kempski put together a list of five possible head coach candidates on November fourth, in case the Eagles moved on from Sirianni after the season. He made it very clear the list was hypothetical and, quote, not necessarily endorsements. End quote.

Here are the five, according to PhillyVoice.

Declan Doyle, thirty, the Baltimore Ravens’ offensive coordinator. He became the NFL’s youngest offensive coordinator at twenty-eight with the 2025 Bears under Ben Johnson, and now he shares play-calling duties in Baltimore with head coach Jesse Minter. Kempski called him a big gamble because of his age.

Kliff Kingsbury, forty-seven, the Los Angeles Rams’ assistant head coach. He was the Cardinals’ head coach from 2019 to 2022, going twenty-eight, thirty-seven and one. He was Washington’s offensive coordinator in 2024 and 2025, where he developed Jayden Daniels. And according to PhillyVoice, the Eagles interviewed him for their offensive coordinator job before 2024 and were reportedly close to a deal.

Klay Kubiak, thirty-eight, the San Francisco 49ers’ offensive coordinator under Kyle Shanahan. According to PhillyVoice, the 49ers rank first in offensive DVOA in 2026.

Nate Scheelhaase, thirty-five, the Rams’ offensive coordinator. The Eagles interviewed him for their offensive coordinator job in 2023. But Sean McVay calls the plays in Los Angeles.

And Grant Udinski, thirty, who reached the second round of the Browns’ head coaching interviews.

According to Kempski, the Eagles would likely want an offensive-minded coach, partly because Vic Fangio could come back as defensive coordinator in 2027. That makes a lot of sense. The defense has a foundation, with Jalen Carter, Moro Ojomo, Quinyon Mitchell and Cooper DeJean. The offense is the problem.

Let me pause here. Comment number one if you would want Udinski. Comment number two if you would want Kubiak. Comment number three if you would rather keep Sirianni.

Now, let me be fair to Nick Sirianni, because he deserves it.

According to ESPN, Sirianni made the playoffs in each of his first five seasons, reached two Super Bowls and won the title after the 2024 season. According to Heavy, his record now stands at sixty-one wins and thirty-three losses. And according to Ed Kracz of Eagles On SI, he is five and zero coming out of bye weeks in his career, including a thirty-five to thirteen win over these same Steelers in 2022.

According to the Associated Press, Sirianni said during the bye, quote, my job is to do everything I can do to turn this around and fix this. End quote. And Jason Kelce said on 94 WIP this week, according to Heavy, quote, I think it’s less about wins and losses and more about improvement. End quote.

Here is my take, and this is just my opinion, not a report. I understand why fans are dreaming about the next Sean McVay. This offense has been painful to watch. But I would be very careful about falling in love with a thirty-year-old coordinator who does not call his own plays. Sirianni has a Super Bowl ring and five straight playoff trips. The final eight games should decide this, not a radio segment. If the offense shows real improvement, Sirianni should get a chance to fix it. If it does not, then the Eagles can start that search, and names like Udinski and Kubiak will be on the list.

Now, let me move to the second big topic, the quarterback position.

Jalen Hurts’s season is over because of a health issue, and the team has said his prognosis is very good. I am not going to speculate beyond that. But with Joe Milton, Tanner McKee and rookie Cole Payton still trying to prove themselves, a lot of people are wondering whether Howie Roseman will take a quarterback in the 2027 draft. And according to PhillyVoice, with two first-round picks, including a likely top-ten pick of their own, the Eagles might have, quote, no choice but take a QB in the draft. End quote. That is the writer’s opinion. But it is a real conversation.

And according to Devon Platana of Eagles On SI, in an article published on November fourteenth, there are three quarterbacks the Eagles should already be watching. And these are not the obvious names at the top of the draft. These are players who could be there on Day 2.

The first is Nico Iamaleava from UCLA. According to Eagles On SI, he is six foot five and two hundred fifteen pounds. Across his time at Tennessee and UCLA, he has completed five hundred twenty-one of eight hundred eighteen passes, sixty-three point seven percent, for five thousand eight hundred forty yards, thirty-eight touchdowns and sixteen interceptions. He has also run for one thousand seventy-eight yards and eleven touchdowns on two hundred eighty-three carries. But according to Eagles On SI, he tore his ACL in a seventeen to fifteen win over Oregon, which ended his 2026 season. And that injury could be part of why he might still be available once the second round begins.

The second is Jayden Maiava from USC. According to Eagles On SI, last season he led the Big Ten in passing yards with three thousand seven hundred eleven, along with yards per completion and passing yards per game. This season, through nine starts, he has two thousand five hundred twenty-four passing yards and twenty touchdowns, with multiple touchdown passes in six of nine games. And according to Eagles On SI, he has fifteen rushing touchdowns, though at only two point four yards per carry. But there are some concerns. According to Eagles On SI, he threw one interception in his first four games and four in his last five. And he is described as a projected second-round pick in most mock drafts.

And the third is Dante Moore from Oregon. According to Eagles On SI, over the last two seasons and nineteen games, he has thrown for four thousand four hundred ninety-one yards, thirty-nine touchdowns and ten interceptions, completing seventy-one point four percent of his passes. He was once projected as the number two pick in the 2026 draft before he returned to Oregon for another season. But he suffered a season-ending concussion in September, and the article notes that he lacks mobility. According to Eagles On SI, he could be a potential Day 2 target.

Now, I want to be careful with injuries here too, because these are young men, and I wish all of them a full recovery. But from a draft perspective, Platana’s point is interesting. If the Eagles use their first-round picks on the offensive line, the defense or a weapon, they could still take a quarterback on Day 2 with real upside.

And this is just my opinion, not a report. Taking a quarterback does not mean giving up on Jalen Hurts. It means planning for the future. The best organizations always have a young quarterback developing. And with ten picks in the first two rounds of the next three drafts, the Eagles can afford to take a swing.

Now, the third topic, and this one is actually good news for the Steelers game.

According to Ed Kracz of Eagles On SI, DeVonta Smith has played in just four games this season because of a hamstring injury, and he is likely to return after the bye. And Dontayvion Wicks, who has missed three straight games with an ankle injury, is also likely to return after the bye. According to Kracz, neither of them was placed on injured reserve, so both can come back at any time.

And look at what they have done when healthy. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, Smith has twenty-six catches for three hundred ten yards and two touchdowns in those four games. Wicks has nineteen catches for two hundred eighty-four yards and one touchdown. And rookie Makai Lemon has thirty-seven catches for three hundred twelve yards and one touchdown, while Darius Cooper has four touchdown catches.

That matters so much for Joe Milton. In his first start against the Giants, he completed eighteen of thirty-four passes for one hundred sixty-nine yards and was sacked seven times. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, his receivers that day were Lemon, Cooper, Hollywood Brown and Elijah Moore. Getting Smith and Wicks back would give him two real weapons he did not have the first time.

And it explains the Hollywood Brown move. According to ESPN sources, as reported by Yardbarker, Brown asked for his release, and the Eagles pointed to the expected returns of Smith and Wicks, plus the improved play of Cooper and Lemon.

According to Kracz, the Eagles now have four open roster spots after trading Jordan Davis and releasing Jaydon Blue, Hollywood Brown and Fred Johnson. And Kracz wrote, quote, Philadelphia might not be done making roster moves. End quote.

So who could fill those spots? According to Kracz, Tank Bigsby, who has been on injured reserve since October sixth with a core muscle injury, and Will Shipley, on injured reserve since October sixteenth with a partial plantar fascia tear, have both missed the required four games. Either one could return for the Steelers game. And practice squad players like offensive lineman Kiran Amegadjie, a six foot five, three hundred twenty-six pound lineman from Yale, could also be promoted. Kracz also mentioned rookie seventh-round picks Cole Wisnieski and Keyshawn James-Newby as young players who could get more snaps over the final eight games.

And Kracz went even deeper. He wrote that Wisnieski getting more playing time could put veteran safety Marcus Epps in jeopardy, and that James-Newby could take snaps from Arnold Ebiketie or A.J. Epenesa. Remember, Ebiketie was almost traded to Cincinnati at the deadline before the Bengals backed out. Kracz also named Uar Bernard and Josh Weru as young players who could get opportunities over the final eight games. That is the theme of this whole week. Every snap in the second half of this season is an audition for 2027.

And here is a fun little note from Kracz about Hollywood Brown. He wore number zero, and since the NFL started allowing that number, no Eagle has kept it for more than one season. D’Andre Swift wore it in 2023, Bryce Huff in 2024, Josh Uche in 2025, and now Brown in 2026. Four years, four different players, and none of them made it to a second season in that jersey. Maybe the next player who wants number zero in Philadelphia should think twice.

So if you want real good news, it is this. The Eagles could get two starting receivers back, possibly a running back back, and a few young players a chance to show what they can do, all in the same week.

And that brings us to Pittsburgh. According to the Inquirer, the Eagles host the Steelers on Sunday, November twenty-second, at one in the afternoon Eastern on CBS. The Steelers are six and two. Then the Eagles travel to Dallas for Thanksgiving on November twenty-sixth.

Here is what I will be watching. First, whether Smith and Wicks are both active. Second, whether Bigsby or Shipley is activated to help Saquon Barkley. Third, who fills the four open roster spots. Fourth, whether Joe Milton looks more comfortable with real weapons around him. And fifth, whether the self-scout produces the kind of offensive improvement that would quiet all of this head coach talk.

On one side, the people who want a new head coach. They will say seven straight losses and an offense ranked near the bottom of the league are reasons enough for a change. On the other side, the people who think this is all moving too fast. They will say Udinski is thirty years old and does not call the plays in Jacksonville. They will say Sirianni has a Super Bowl ring, two Super Bowl trips and five straight playoff appearances. They will say no coach could survive losing his starting quarterback, his right tackle, his center and his top receivers in one season. They will say Smith and Wicks coming back could change how the offense looks. And they will say Sirianni is five and zero after bye weeks, so give him the final eight games before making any decision.

And here is where I land. This is just my opinion, not a report. Grant Udinski is an exciting name, and I love that he is a Bucks County kid. If the Eagles ever do make a change, he deserves to be on the list. But right now, Nick Sirianni is the head coach, there is no trade, and there is no new hire. The most important thing this week is getting DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks back, giving Joe Milton a real chance, and seeing whether this team looks different after the bye. And on the quarterback question, I would absolutely take a swing on a Day 2 quarterback in 2027. Iamaleava’s size and athleticism would be the most interesting to me.
