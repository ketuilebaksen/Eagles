Let me start with the draft.

According to PhillyVoice, the Eagles selected Eli Stowers out of Vanderbilt with the fifty-fourth overall pick in the second round of the 2026 NFL Draft. And a pick like that is never an accident. When a team uses a second-round pick on a tight end, it is not looking for someone to fill out the roster. It is looking for a future starter.

And Stowers’ path to the NFL is one of the most interesting in this draft class. According to PhillyVoice, he was a high school quarterback. He enrolled at Texas A&M and played quarterback there in 2021 and 2022. Then he transferred to New Mexico State and switched to tight end in 2023, catching thirty-five passes for three hundred sixty-six yards and two touchdowns. Then he moved on to Vanderbilt. In 2024, he caught forty-nine passes for six hundred thirty-eight yards and five touchdowns. And in 2025, he caught sixty-two passes for seven hundred sixty-nine yards and four touchdowns. According to Larry Rupp of Eagles On SI, he is a John Mackey Award winner, the award given to the best tight end in college football.

And then there is the athleticism. According to PhillyVoice, he is listed at six foot four and two hundred thirty-nine pounds. He ran a four point five one forty-yard dash. And he broke records with a forty-five and a half inch vertical leap and a one hundred thirty-five inch broad jump. Eagles Nation, those are numbers you usually see from wide receivers, not tight ends. A tight end who can jump like that is a nightmare in the red zone.

According to PhillyVoice, the scouting report praised his yards-after-catch ability, and noted that Vanderbilt used him in a lot of different alignments to create mismatches. The main concern was his blocking, and PhillyVoice described him as more of a big receiver for now. The plan, according to that report, was for him to develop for a year behind Dallas Goedert.

And his story is even better than his numbers. According to Dave Zangaro of NBC Sports Philadelphia, Stowers was a highly recruited high school quarterback from Denton, Texas. But at Texas A&M, he tore his labrum and needed surgery, and it ended his quarterback career. He said, quote, I couldn’t throw the ball the same after the shoulder injury. End quote. So he transferred to New Mexico State, where he lost the starting quarterback job in 2023 to Diego Pavia, who went on to become a Heisman Trophy finalist. And instead of giving up, Stowers told offensive coordinator Tim Beck he would do anything to help the team. Beck suggested he try tight end. When Beck left for Vanderbilt in 2024, Pavia and Stowers followed him. And the rest is history.

According to the team’s official website, Stowers was a First-Team All-American and First-Team All-SEC selection. He was the first Vanderbilt player ever to win the John Mackey Award. And he also won the William V. Campbell Trophy, known as the Academic Heisman, with a three point nine two grade point average. According to the team, Pro Football Focus gave him an overall grade of eighty-one point six in 2025, seventh among one hundred ninety-nine tight ends in the country, and a receiving grade of eighty-five point three, sixth in the nation. For his college career, he finished with one hundred eleven catches for one thousand four hundred seven yards and eleven touchdowns.

And the Eagles were thrilled to get him. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, Nick Sirianni said, quote, he is a tough cover. Catches everything, good after the catch, speed, quickness. End quote. According to the team, Sirianni also said, quote, the guy is super talented. Athletically, he does some things that nobody else can do. End quote. Howie Roseman said, quote, we have a lot of confidence in our coaches and our staff to develop players. End quote. And Stowers himself said, quote, it’s just such a blessing to be a part of this team. End quote.

And the long-term plan was always clear. According to NBC Sports Philadelphia, Dallas Goedert came back for 2026 on a one-year, seven million dollar deal, and the Eagles hope Stowers becomes Goedert’s long-term replacement. Stowers even said he watched Goedert during his own move to tight end. And he explained why playing quarterback helped him so much. Quote, I understood the whole scope of the offense, because you have to as a quarterback. End quote.

And think about the draft class Stowers came in with, because it matters for this whole conversation. According to the team, the Eagles traded up three spots with Dallas to select wide receiver Makai Lemon at number twenty in the first round. Then they took Stowers at fifty-four in the second round. And according to NBC Sports Philadelphia, they took offensive tackle Markel Bell from Miami with the sixty-eighth pick in the third round. According to Zangaro, Stowers was only the second tight end taken in the whole draft, behind Kenyon Sadiq, who went sixteenth overall. Lemon, Stowers and Bell were all early picks on offense, and all three of them are now getting real NFL snaps as rookies. That is a big part of what the second half of this season is really about. Lemon leads the team in receiving yards, and Bell has taken over at right tackle since Lane Johnson retired. Stowers is the one who has had to wait the longest. And that is exactly why so many people want to see him get his chance now.

Now, here is where the plan ran into trouble.

According to Ed Kracz of Eagles On SI, Stowers was placed on injured reserve forty-eight hours before the September thirteenth season opener with a quad injury, and his hamstring also was not one hundred percent. He spent about six weeks on injured reserve. According to the team, he was activated from injured reserve in late October, along with Landon Dickerson and Grant Calcaterra.

And that is a big deal for a rookie. Every practice is a chance to learn the system, fix mistakes and build chemistry with the quarterback. While other rookies were stacking up reps, Stowers had to put all of his energy into getting healthy. And I am not going to speculate about his health beyond what has been reported. But it is fair to say he started his NFL career behind.

According to Kracz, offensive coordinator Sean Mannion said Stowers had been, quote, working his tail off to get healthy. End quote. And Mannion also said he is, quote, mentally really, really sharp. End quote.

And you can see why he picked things up quickly. Remember, he played quarterback in college. According to Kracz, Stowers said, quote, I think that there’s a lot of carryover mentally from the quarterback standpoint. End quote. A tight end who thinks like a quarterback can understand coverages, find soft spots in a zone and know where the ball is supposed to go. That is a real advantage.

Now, let me walk you through his first three games.

According to Kracz, Stowers made his NFL debut against the Dallas Cowboys on Monday Night Football. He was targeted once and did not make a catch. Not the debut anyone dreamed of. But after everything he went through to get there, just stepping on the field in a rivalry game on a national stage was a step forward.

Then came Washington. According to Kracz, Stowers caught all three of his targets for fifty-three yards against the Commanders, including a thirty-four-yard catch on the final play of the twenty-seven to ten loss. According to Larry Rupp, he did that on just thirteen offensive snaps. And according to Glenn Erby of Eagles Wire, as published on Yahoo Sports, the offense managed only two hundred sixty-eight net yards in that game, and Stowers’ fifty-three yards were roughly one quarter of Tanner McKee’s passing total.

After that game, according to Kracz, Stowers said, quote, it’s always good to get the first one out of the way. End quote. And, quote, I think it was a good first step for me. End quote.

And then the Giants game. According to Kracz, Stowers played just seven offensive snaps, while Dallas Goedert played fifty-seven, which is eighty-one percent. But even in limited snaps, he showed something. Eagles analyst Fran Duffy posted, quote, second week in a row we are seeing Eli Stowers, number eighty-seven, give this kind of effort blocking a CB. End quote. And according to Rupp, the caption on that post was, quote, baby steps. End quote.

Think about that. The one big concern about Stowers coming out of college was his blocking. And now, in two straight games, people are noticing his effort as a blocker. That is exactly the kind of growth you want to see from a rookie.

Let me pause here. Comment number one if you want to see Stowers get at least twenty snaps against the Steelers. Comment number two if you think the Eagles should keep easing him in.

Now, here is the big question. How much should the Eagles play him over the final eight games?

According to Ed Kracz, in an article published on November twelfth about five necessary changes the Eagles must make after the bye, one of them is to build a role for Eli Stowers. Kracz argued that Goedert’s workload should start shifting to Stowers, because Goedert is unlikely to come back on another one-year deal. He also questioned why Johnny Mundt and Grant Calcaterra are still on the roster.

And Larry Rupp of Eagles On SI made a similar case before the deadline. He wrote that Stowers was expected to contribute right away, but injuries limited him and Goedert stayed ahead of him on the depth chart. According to Rupp, Goedert is thirty-one and on an expiring contract, and a bigger role for Stowers would let the Eagles evaluate him as a possible starter.

And according to Glenn Erby of Eagles Wire, Stowers is more than a traditional in-line tight end. He could line up in the slot, off the line of scrimmage, or in two-tight-end sets next to Goedert. That way, he does not have to take Goedert’s job to get on the field. The Eagles can use both of them together and create mismatches against linebackers and safeties.

Here is my take, and this is just my opinion, not a report. I would give Stowers a lot more snaps, starting against the Steelers. Not because Goedert is playing badly, but because the Eagles need answers about the future. According to the Inquirer, Goedert stayed at the deadline partly because trading him would have roughly doubled his dead cap to eighteen point two million dollars. He took a pay cut to stay in 2025 and came back on a one-year deal this year. He is a professional, and he will help Stowers. But the Eagles need to know by January whether Stowers can be their starting tight end in 2027.

But I also want to be fair about the risk. Stowers is not a rookie who went through a normal training camp and built up his body for a full season. He missed a lot of time with a quad and hamstring. So the Eagles have to find the right balance. Push him, but do not overload him. Give him real game reps against real NFL defenders, but manage his snaps the smart way.

And there is one more story at tight end. According to Kracz, Zach Ertz has five hundred eighty-two career catches, just six short of Harold Carmichael’s franchise record of five hundred eighty-nine. That is a real milestone for one of the most important players in Eagles history. So the Eagles have three tight ends with three very different stories. Goedert, the veteran leader. Ertz, the legend chasing history. And Stowers, the rookie trying to prove he belongs. Finding snaps for all three is a challenge. But it is a good problem to have.

Now, Stowers is not the only young player who should be getting a long look. Kracz listed several more in his five changes.

The first is rookie wide receiver Makai Lemon. According to Kracz, Lemon leads the Eagles in receiving yards with three hundred twelve, two more than DeVonta Smith. And since Week 7, he has had three straight games with at least six catches and fifty-plus yards. Kracz wants the Eagles to keep feeding him.

The second is defensive tackle Ty Robinson. According to Kracz, Robinson has only sixty-one defensive snaps in his second season, but he has held up well in a rotational role. With Jordan Davis traded, there should be more opportunities. And according to Rupp, Robinson is six foot five and two hundred eighty-eight pounds, with an explosive first step. At Nebraska, he was described as a wrecking ball.

The third is defensive tackle Zion Wilson. According to Kracz, Wilson was waived on cutdown day despite a strong preseason, with nine tackles, two tackles for loss or no gain, and four quarterback pressures in three games. He signed with the practice squad on October thirteenth, and he had seven sacks at East Carolina in 2025. Kracz suggested elevating him or signing him to the active roster to help replace Davis.

And finally, Kracz wants the Eagles to evaluate their other rookies. He suggested that rookie quarterback Cole Payton should play the final two games, no matter how Joe Milton does. He also wants more time for outside linebacker Keyshawn James-Newby, safety Cole Wisniewski and guard Micah Morris, who is six foot five and three hundred thirty-five pounds. And he questioned why veteran safety Marcus Epps is still playing.

And there is more good news. According to Kracz, DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks are both likely to return after the bye, and neither was placed on injured reserve. Tank Bigsby and Will Shipley have both missed the required four games on injured reserve, so either one could return for the Steelers game. And with Jordan Davis traded and Jaydon Blue, Hollywood Brown and Fred Johnson released, the Eagles have four open roster spots.

So think about what the offense could look like against Pittsburgh. Smith and Wicks back at receiver. Lemon still growing. Darius Cooper, who has four touchdown catches, according to NBC Sports Philadelphia. Goedert and Stowers together at tight end. And maybe Bigsby or Shipley next to Saquon Barkley in the backfield. That is a lot more talent than Joe Milton had against the Giants, when he was sacked seven times.

Now, let me remind you about this Steelers game. According to the Inquirer, it is on Sunday, November twenty-second, at one in the afternoon Eastern on CBS. According to Kracz, it was originally scheduled for a national four twenty-five window before it was flexed because of how the Eagles have played. The Steelers are six and two. And according to Kracz, Nick Sirianni is five and zero coming out of bye weeks in his career, including a thirty-five to thirteen win over Pittsburgh in 2022.

Here is what I will be watching. First, how many snaps Eli Stowers gets, and whether the Eagles use him in two-tight-end sets with Goedert. Second, whether Smith and Wicks are back. Third, whether Ty Robinson and Zion Wilson get more snaps on the defensive line. Fourth, whether Bigsby or Shipley is activated. And fifth, whether Zach Ertz can get closer to Harold Carmichael’s record.

They will say his blocking effort is getting better every week, just like Fran Duffy pointed out. They will say Goedert is thirty-one and on an expiring deal, and the Eagles need to know what they have in Stowers before the offseason. Let him use that quarterback brain and that four point five one speed against linebackers. And keep building his blocking, because that is what will turn him from a big receiver into a complete tight end. If he shows real growth over the final eight games, the Eagles might already have their next starting tight end. And in a season like this one, that is the kind of good news Eagles fans need.
